MASARI

Where Excellence Takes Center Stage

An AI-powered academic advisor and centralized intelligence dashboard bridging the gap between fragmented computer science learning and real-world employment.

The Origin Story

MASARI was born from the strategic merger of two distinct, powerful systems to eliminate student friction.

AI Course Recommender

Initially designed as an independent intelligence dashboard focused purely on parsing student intent and recommending highly-targeted computer science courses.

ElZatona Interview System

A dedicated AI-driven mock interview environment built to challenge candidates with dynamic technical questions and evaluate their performance.

The Unified Vision: MASARI

We recognized a critical flaw: students were still overwhelmed by disjointed tools, conflicted on where to begin, and intimidated by the complex workflow spanning learning to employment. By merging these two powerful engines, MASARI was forged into a single, cohesive ecosystem—providing an all-in-one, frictionless journey from the first course to the final job offer.

Project Overview

What It Is

A centralized, intelligent platform combining an NLP-based course recommender, an ATS resume optimizer, and an AI interview simulator.

What It Achieves

It personalizes the student learning journey, transforms weak resumes into ATS-compliant profiles, and simulates realistic job interviews with actionable feedback.

Why It Matters

CS students currently face a fragmented landscape. MASARI eliminates the guesswork, helping students focus on learning and landing dream roles rather than navigating chaos.

The Fragmented Journey

Thousands of disjointed courses and disconnected tools leave students without a cohesive path to success.

Keyword Search Fails

Traditional search methods fail to understand the true intent or current skill level of the student.

ATS Black-Holes

Highly qualified students get rejected by Applicant Tracking Systems due to poorly optimized resumes.

Generic Interview Prep

Static lists of interview questions do not adapt to a candidate's weaknesses or specific job descriptions.

The Solution Engines

A deep dive into the core capabilities powering MASARI.

NLP Course Recommender

Intelligent matching eliminating the guesswork from upskilling.

TF-IDF Vectorization

Employs TF-IDF with bigram support for context-aware search, ranking courses based on true semantic relevance rather than just keyword matching.

AI-Powered Ratings

Uses NLTK VADER Sentiment Analysis to score course descriptions and reviews, heavily weighting real student feedback (70%).

Prestige Bonus

Courses from elite institutions (MIT, Google, Stanford, IBM) receive algorithmic boosts to guarantee high-quality results.

NLP Summarization

Extracts and tokenizes reviews to generate human-readable summaries of course pros, cons, and difficulty.

ATS Optimization

Transforming weak resumes into ATS-compliant, high-scoring profiles.

Deep Entity Extraction

Parses PDF resumes and extracts critical entities (skills, experience, education) to compare directly against a target job description.

Missing Keyword Analysis

Instantly highlights critical missing keywords required by modern Applicant Tracking Systems to pass the initial screening phase.

Actionable Re-writes

Provides generative AI suggestions to rewrite bullet points, maximizing impact using strong action verbs and quantifiable metrics.

AI Interview Simulator

A multi-modal environment for rigorous technical and behavioral evaluation.

Technical Evaluation

Features Ace Editor for live coding. The LLM acts as a strict interviewer, explicitly grading on Time Complexity (Big-O), Space Complexity, and edge cases.

Voice & STAR Method

Integrates WebRTC & Whisper-Large-V3 to record/transcribe answers instantly, grading them strictly against the STAR framework.

Generative Search

Bypasses static databases by dynamically generating hyper-relevant theoretical and behavioral questions for any CS topic.

Progress Radar

Historical progress securely logged to MongoDB, allowing users to track proficiency growth across categories via Chart.js.

AI Study Planner & Dashboard

Intelligently structuring the learning journey.

Generative Curriculum

Maps out an ideal, week-by-week curriculum and roadmap tailored specifically to the user's availability and career targets.

Progress Tracking

Integrated with secure user profiles, allowing students to save plans, check off milestones, and stay accountable.

Intelligence EDA Dashboard

Provides deep market & skill visualizations using Plotly and Chart.js, helping users understand trending tech stacks and high-demand salaries to drive career decisions.

Methodology

A structured 5-step design process driven by advanced NLP.

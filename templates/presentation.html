MASARI

Where Excellence Takes Center Stage

An AI-powered academic advisor and centralized intelligence dashboard bridging the gap between fragmented computer science learning and real-world employment.

The Origin Story

MASARI was born from the strategic merger of two distinct, powerful systems to eliminate student friction.

AI Course Recommender

Initially designed as an independent intelligence dashboard focused purely on parsing student intent and recommending highly-targeted computer science courses.

ElZatona Interview System

A dedicated AI-driven mock interview environment built to challenge candidates with dynamic technical questions and evaluate their performance.

The Unified Vision: MASARI

We recognized a critical flaw: students were still overwhelmed by disjointed tools, conflicted on where to begin, and intimidated by the complex workflow spanning learning to employment. By merging these two powerful engines, MASARI was forged into a single, cohesive ecosystem—providing an all-in-one, frictionless journey from the first course to the final job offer.

Project Overview

What It Is

A centralized, intelligent platform combining an NLP-based course recommender, an ATS resume optimizer, and an AI interview simulator.

What It Achieves

It personalizes the student learning journey, transforms weak resumes into ATS-compliant profiles, and simulates realistic job interviews with actionable feedback.

Why It Matters

CS students currently face a fragmented landscape. MASARI eliminates the guesswork, helping students focus on learning and landing dream roles rather than navigating chaos.

The Fragmented Journey

Thousands of disjointed courses and disconnected tools leave students without a cohesive path to success.

Keyword Search Fails

Traditional search methods fail to understand the true intent or current skill level of the student.

ATS Black-Holes

Highly qualified students get rejected by Applicant Tracking Systems due to poorly optimized resumes.

Generic Interview Prep

Static lists of interview questions do not adapt to a candidate's weaknesses or specific job descriptions.
